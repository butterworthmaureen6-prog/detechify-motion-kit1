# Transitions (v0.2 DRAFT)

Transitions are camera flows or in-world events. No graphic wipes.

| Name | Trigger | How |
|---|---|---|
| `flow-follow` | Moving from one object or system to another | Follow a packet along a cable into the next scene |
| `screen-dive` | Going inside a program, network or chip | Push into a monitor or port until it fills the frame, then reveal the inside |
| `rise-reveal` | Scale or "bigger picture" beat | Pull back and up from a detail to the whole environment |
| `light-shift` | Calm to tension, or tension to explanation | Cross-fade light preset on the same scene over 12-20 frames |
| `flash-hit` | A hook sentence landing, a breach, a failure | 2-4 frame white or red flash with a short shake |
| `motion-blur-cut` | Last resort | Hide a hard cut inside a fast move |
| `section-card` | Long-form chapter break | Dark card, one large word, wordmark bottom-right, 20-30 frames |

## Rules
- Hard cuts are rare. If used, hide them in motion or a flash.
- One transition per beat. Do not stack.
- Match the transition to the story: breach = flash-hit, going deeper = screen-dive, new idea = flow-follow.
