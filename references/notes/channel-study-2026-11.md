# Channel study (our own notes, 2026-11-05)

Method: opened each channel's video list, played one IT-relevant video per channel, paused at sampled timestamps and looked at the frames (about 480p). No frames are stored in this repo. Notes are our own words.

## Branch Education (primary look)
- Studied: "How does Computer Cache, Memory, and Storage Work?" (youtube.com/watch?v=TfhL5kBiQVI), at roughly 3, 10 and 18 minutes.
- Channel page says models are built in Blender (Eevee and Cycles).
- Dark slate-blue studio void. Glossy floor with faint grid lines.
- Macro-detail hardware: green PCB, copper, layered silicon dice, cutaways seen from low angles.
- Data shown as streams of tiny light particles pouring into components.
- Translucent blue spec table with row icons and product cut-outs below.
- Layers stacked and offset to show structure (cutaway stacks fading into the distance).
- Small logo in the bottom-right corner. Captions at the bottom.

## Big Data Factor (borrowed: scale ladder)
- Studied: "True Scale of Data Storage" (youtube.com/watch?v=i1SiWE7E48Q).
- Real-world environment (warehouse floor with safety markings) used to make size believable.
- Glowing blue cubes labelled BYTE, then groups of them, growing in steps.
- A labelled unit ladder down the left edge with the current unit highlighted.
- Low camera angles, shallow depth of field, motion blur during fast moves.
- The larger units are shown as a huge monolith towering under open sky.

## Animagraffs (borrowed: diagram mode)
- Studied: "How a VPN Works" (youtube.com/watch?v=sr2-K6AaHNI).
- Bright, colour-coded diorama: red zone = ISP, blue zone = VPN, white and green ground planes.
- Toy-like but recognisable devices (phone, router, laptop) with address labels on them.
- Data drawn as ribbon streams, with labelled packets.
- A hand holds the phone to ground the scene in a human action.
- Slightly tilted, near-isometric camera, steady and readable.

## What we took
- Branch: studio void, glossy floor, hardware detail, light-as-data, glass spec table.
- Big Data Factor: scale ladder, true-scale staging.
- Animagraffs: colour-coded zones and address labels for network flows.

## Limits of this study
- Sampled frames only: no motion timing, no audio.
- Low resolution: good for look and composition, not pixel measurement.
- Next: sample more frames per video at short intervals to measure how long camera moves last.
