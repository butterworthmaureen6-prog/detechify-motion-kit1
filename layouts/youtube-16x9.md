# Layout: YouTube (16:9, 1920x1080)

## Safe zones
- Keep key content inside x: 96-1824 and y: 54-1026 (5% margins).
- Bottom corners may be covered by player controls and end cards.

## Composition
- Use the width: hero object on one side, glass panel or diagram on the other third.
- Wide establishing shots first, then close hardware detail, then a pull-back reveal.
- Glass panel sits on the right at 14% from the top (as in `server-hall`).
- Scale ladder runs down the left edge.

## Structure
- Build each video from scenes of 10-40 s. Each scene = one component set, one light preset, one idea.
- Change scene (or light preset) when the idea changes. Hand off by flow (see `motion/transitions.md`).
- Section breaks: dark card with one large word and the channel wordmark bottom-right.

## Output
- 1920x1080 at 30 fps **[TUNE]**.
- Leave 1-2 s of calm at the end of each scene so edits have room.
